# Implemented versus planned

## Working core in v0.1.0

- Separate offline Windows/PyQt6 application and SQLite identity.
- Nine lessons with sounded, rendered examples, guided entry and sequential checks.
- C-major one-pitch rhythm, two-pitch bridge, three-pitch integration, eighths, wider pitch sets, dotted values/rests and sixteenth cells.
- One to four bars, editable BPM and hearing allowance.
- Clickable staff with whole through sixteenth values, dots, rests, accidentals, ties, unknown pitch/duration, selection, dragging, insert/delete, undo/redo and keyboard entry.
- Beam groups within beats, flags, ledger lines and accidental carry within a measure.
- Separate pitch, attack, duration/rest and coarse notation grades; omission-tolerant pitch alignment.
- Hearing snapshots, confidence, assistance records and duplicate protection.
- Beat/subdivision/dropout/count-in/no-click modes, with no timed visual pulse.
- Paper entry or clearly labeled self-check; matching-ID WAV and blank PDF; separate answer-key PDF.
- Matched-rhythm follow-up, exploratory easier/stay/challenge controls, conservative one-step promotion and local review reminders.
- Light theme, system display scaling, default Vocal rest, backup/restore and evidence export.

## Next: fluency and transfer

- Larger coherent motif and cadence library, including less predictable endings.
- Truly separate skill models for pulse, duration, subdivision, extraction, scale degrees, integration, retention and no-click transfer.
- Automatic unfamiliar delayed-review scheduling across days and retained-learning evidence.
- Comparable baseline/follow-up benchmark UI, strategy comparisons and richer confidence calibration.
- Instructor-configurable keys, meters, clefs, allowed rhythms and first-note/reference policy.
- True beat-grid placement, rhythm-cell lesson isolation, finer note-entry accessibility and engraving of long cross-bar events.
- Automatic classroom playback rounds and timed writing intervals.
- Separate scale-degree, register and accidental feedback; more nuanced rhythm alignment for local shifts.
- User-facing threshold controls, high-contrast theme and text-size setting.
- Licensed sampled piano option; broader physical output-device testing.

## Later: broader dictation

- Minor conventions, compound meter lessons, pickups, tuplets, syncopation and chromaticism.
- Optional explicit local recording and self-check after Vocal rest is off, followed by independently validated scoring.
- Sounded harmonic listening and functional progression work.
- Two-part notation and per-voice timing/pitch assessment.

The pure score math already tests 3/4 and 6/8 timing. That does not mean a complete compound-meter course or corresponding UI has been shipped. No empty advanced-module screens are presented as finished features.
